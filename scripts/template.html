Open Area 2 — the rule

Southern edge: a straight line due east from Grafton to the coast (37.4 km), with the whole of Grafton pulled inside by a ring-fence drawn on the road network (570 km²).

Western edge: held at Grafton's distance from the coast — 35.0 km — all the way north to the Queensland border.

The road-snapped variant follows the Summerland Way, Casino–Coraki Road, Bruxner Highway, Kyogle Road, Nimbin Road and Route 32/34.

Grafton, South Grafton, Junction Hill, Clarenza, Great Marlow, Waterview Heights and Ulmarra are all inside the ring-fence.
