How the road boundaries were built

Shortest-path routing along OpenStreetMap road centrelines between anchors set on the geometric line. Where no connected road network exists — the Sunraysia mallee, the Snowy Mountains, the far south-east forests — the boundary stays on the geometric line rather than being dragged off-corridor.
